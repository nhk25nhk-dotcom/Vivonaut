# AI use log – Vivonaut

**Project:** Vivonaut – Your Intelligent Health Companion in Space (NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, Universal Event)
**Team:** Vivonaut
**Version:** 15 November 2026 (final record, build up to Phase 10A)

## Disclosure statement

This project was built with assistance from Claude (Anthropic), an AI assistant. Concept, research direction, design decisions, testing and review by Team Vivonaut. Code and text generated by AI were reviewed and tested by the team. The application itself uses no AI service while it runs: it works offline with a rule-based engine and prepared text, and Viva's voice is the browser's built-in text-to-speech, not an AI-generated voice.

## How the work was organised

- **Build phases (Phases 0 to 10)** ran in a Claude Project on the Claude Pro account of team member Emilia Staffiero. Each phase returned a numbered `index_vN.html` and an updated `STATE.md`; no one edited the code by hand.
- **Project support** (documents, submission checklist, independent browser tests of every build, review of wording and sources) ran in a separate Claude chat on the Team Owner's account.
- All dates below are 14 November 2026 (the hackathon day) unless stated. Phase 10A finished just after midnight on 15 November.

## Where AI-generated content is declared

## What was not generated by AI

- The decision to build Vivonaut, its identity and the review decisions (the team, with the Team Owner's final say). The safety rules and the project documents were drafted with AI assistance and then decided, checked and approved by the team.
- The scientific source selection and verification (team science members).
- The demo crew, readings and events are fictional and simulated; the application contains no real medical data and no NASA datasets. NASA resources are cited as references and are listed in `docs/RESOURCES.md`.
